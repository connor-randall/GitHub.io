// a civilization nobody scripted //
Each world is a simulated settlement founded by 100 people. Nobody writes its story: its people eat, work, fall in love, have children, open and lose businesses, and die, all through simple rules for food, money, prices, families and health. There are no scripted events and no AI. Each world runs until nobody is left alive, then the next one (World 2, World 3, ...) begins. One simulated day passes every 30 seconds, and this page follows it live.
