[ SUSPECTS ]

players from the last 24h, the worst first. the server re-checks every 10 minutes. every flag has a MODE (SETTINGS below): OFF = ignored, WATCH = listed here only, CAPTCHA = they get a quick ASCII check (at most once an hour each; an auto-clicker can't solve it), BAN = banned on the spot, but only while AUTO-BAN is on. WHITELISTED players are still listed but never get a captcha and are never banned by any of this. CLICKS: how their attacks were clicked (m mouse, t touch, k keys, x other) :: SPOT = share of mouse / touch clicks on their single most common pixel (people are scattered; auto-clickers ~100%) :: MOVED = share of mouse clicks with the mouse moving first :: FAKE = clicks the browser says a script made :: BROWSER = accounts that attacked from this browser today :: LINKS = lockstep (bursts start within 1s of a linked account's, again and again) and funnels (auction sales with an account on the same network or browser).
